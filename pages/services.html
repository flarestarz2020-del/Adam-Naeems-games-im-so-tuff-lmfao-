Housing in Finland ✈️ Moving

Navigate Oikotie, Vuokraovi and city housing applications

Where to search

Most rentals are listed on Oikotie and Vuokraovi. Good apartments disappear fast — apply within hours of a listing appearing.

Oikotie.fi ↗ Vuokraovi.com ↗

You'll typically need

  • Valid ID / passport
  • Proof of income or benefits
  • Deposit of 1–2 months rent
  • Finnish personal identity code (when available)

City-Owned Housing

Apply for city-owned housing for cheaper rent. Helsinki's programme is managed by Helsingin kaupungin asunnot. Expect long queues but significantly lower costs.

Heka.fi ↗
💡 Pro Tip: Apply for city-owned housing (Helsinki: Helsingin kaupungin asunnot) for cheaper rent — but expect longer waiting queues.

Registration & ID ✈️ Moving

DVV registration, Finnish ID card, bank account setup

Step-by-Step Process

  • 1
    Visit DVV Register your address and get a Finnish personal identity code at Digi- ja väestötietovirasto.
  • 2
    Get your ID card Visit the Police of Finland to get your ID card — needed for almost everything.
  • 3
    Open a bank account Open a bank account (Nordea, OP, etc.) to get your Finnish bank ID for online government services.

Official Resources

Book your appointments online in advance to save time.

DVV.fi ↗ Poliisi.fi ↗ Nordea ↗ OP Ryhmä ↗
💡 Once you have your bank ID, you can do almost everything online — from signing rental contracts to applying for benefits. This saves enormous amounts of time.

Banking & Digital Access ✈️ Moving

Your bank ID is the key to the Finnish digital ecosystem

Why Bank ID matters

Finnish online banking ID (verkkopankkitunnukset) is the standard way to authenticate with government services, sign contracts electronically, and apply for benefits through Kela.

Essential Apps to Download

  • Your bank's mobile app
  • Kela — for benefits management
  • HSL — public transport (Helsinki area)
  • Vero — tax authority
  • OmaKanta — health records

Get a Finnish SIM

Pick up a SIM card from Elisa, DNA or Telia. Prepaid options are available at most supermarkets (K-Market, S-Market, R-Kiosk).

Elisa ↗ DNA ↗

Language Learning ✈️ & 🌲 Both

Finnish & Swedish courses tailored to your daily life

Why Learn Finnish?

Basic Finnish gives you a huge advantage in the job market and daily life. We offer structured courses for all levels.

Available levels: Beginner · Intermediate · Conversational · Business Finnish

Swedish in Finland

Finland has two official languages: Finnish and Swedish. Swedish speakers make up ~5% of the population. Knowing Swedish opens doors in certain regions and workplaces.

Free Public Resources

  • Integration courses through municipalities
  • Yle Areena — Finnish TV & radio

Jobs & Benefits ✈️ Moving

TE Services, Kela benefits and job search strategies

Job Search Portals

  • TE Services (te-palvelut.fi) — register immediately
  • LinkedIn — strong in Finland
  • Oikotie Jobs
  • Monster.fi
  • Company career pages directly
TE-palvelut ↗

Kela Benefits

Register at TE Services immediately upon arrival. Apply to Kela for housing benefit and basic unemployment allowance if needed.

  • Basic unemployment allowance
  • Housing benefit — can cover a large portion of rent
  • General housing allowance
Kela.fi ↗

Reality Check

  • English-only jobs exist but are limited
  • Basic Finnish = big competitive advantage
  • Networking matters — use LinkedIn actively
  • Internships can lead to full employment
⚠️ You must actively apply for jobs or benefit payments may stop. Keep records of all applications.

Healthcare ✈️ & 🌲 Both

Navigate the Finnish healthcare system confidently

Public Healthcare Access

Public healthcare is decent but not always fast. Access it through your local health centre (terveyskeskus) after registration. You'll be assigned a health centre based on your home address.

Kela Card

Your Kela card helps reduce healthcare costs. Apply for it once you have your personal identity code and are registered with Kela.

Get Kela card ↗

Emergency & Digital

  • Emergency: 112
  • Medical helpline: 116 117
  • OmaKanta app — digital health records
  • Terveystalo, Mehiläinen — private options
💡 Use the OmaKanta service (kanta.fi) to view your health records, prescriptions and lab results online using your bank ID.
